# Problem and goals

**Challenge (NASA Space Apps 2026):** *Identify Earth Locations that Analog the
Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars.*
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/permanent-moon-base-locations-and-mars/

## Problem
- Mission teams test hardware, train crews and rehearse operations at Earth
  analog sites before they fly. The best-known examples are the Atacama,
  Haughton Crater and Death Valley.
- The challenge states that these sites are "not entirely well characterized".
- Sites are chosen by reputation, logistics and visual resemblance, not by
  systematic, multi-dimensional comparison with the actual target.
- No place on Earth matches the Moon or Mars in every way, yet sites are rarely
  described in terms of *which* characteristics match, *how well*, and *where
  the analogy breaks down*.
- NASA's global Earth-observation archive could find **previously
  unrecognized** analogs, but nobody screens it against specific base and
  landing sites.

## Who is affected
- Planners and scientists choosing training and test sites for Artemis base
  regions and Mars landing and exploration sites.
- Instrument, rover and ISRU test teams that need specific conditions:
  thermal cycling, slopes, regolith-like surfaces, ice.
- Educators and the public, who see analog sites as a single "looks like Mars"
  idea.

## Goals (v2, by 12 Nov 2026)
1. **Characterize:** score any place on Earth against each target across
   physical dimensions (terrain, climate and aridity, thermal, surface,
   mineralogy, ice and illumination, dust, operations), all from NASA data.
   Show the per-dimension match and where the analogy breaks down.
2. **Discover:** screen all of Earth's land on a global grid and surface
   *novel leads* far from known analog sites.
3. **Target what NASA is planning:**
   - Moon: all 9 NASA Artemis III candidate south-pole regions.
   - Mars: Jezero (the science landing site) and Arcadia Planitia (the ice-rich
     human-base candidate).
4. **Prove it:**
   - Pre-registered validation against NASA-documented analogs, with
     confidence intervals.
   - Honest limits, including Earth's gravity, atmosphere and radiation.
5. **Share it:**
   - An interactive globe app with live scoring of any place.
   - A mission dossier PDF.
   - A written findings report for the public.

## Non-goals
- Replacing field surveys. SolMate produces *leads* and *characterizations*,
  not certifications.
- Scoring caves, mines and subsurface environments. There is no global remote
  data for them; they are listed as context only.
- Deep-learning image matching. It is hard to validate with so few labelled
  sites, and the challenge asks for analysis "beyond visual similarity".
