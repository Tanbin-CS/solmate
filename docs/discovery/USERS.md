# Target users

| Persona | Need | What SolMate gives them |
|---|---|---|
| **Analog mission planner** (NASA/ESA field campaign lead) | Pick a training or test site that best matches a specific Artemis region or Mars site | Ranked sites per target and per dimension; a dossier PDF with matches, gaps, logistics notes and sources |
| **Instrument / rover test engineer** | A site with specific conditions: thermal cycling, slope range, barren regolith-like surface, dust | The "Dimension leaders" view; the Mission-ops preset; "Use it for" lines |
| **ISRU / ice researcher** | Earth places with ground ice in conditions like Arcadia Planitia or polar craters | The ice dimension (NASA POWER mean annual temperature, MODIS snow); SWIM context |
| **Planetary scientist** | Process analogs (aeolian, evaporite, volcanic, periglacial) that are not well studied yet | Process tags; novel leads more than 300 km from known sites |
| **Educator / student / public** | Understand why a place on Earth is like the Moon or Mars, and why it is not | The guided tour, plain-English Findings page and the breaks-down card |
| **Citizen contributor** | Suggest a site they know, with evidence | The nomination form with a review queue |

## Usage assumptions
- Most visitors arrive from the NASA project page, the video or a shared link,
  on desktop or phone, with no account.
- Judges will spend 1–3 minutes, so the "Take the tour" path must show the
  core result with no typing.
- Field planners will spend longer: they compare, adjust weights and export.
